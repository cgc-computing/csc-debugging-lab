## Notes and items the lead may want to double-check

- **Error-message wording.** The SOLUTION files quote Racket's current phrasing ("application: not a procedure; expected a procedure that can be applied to arguments", "car: contract violation expected: pair? given: …", "add: arity mismatch; …"). DrRacket's R5RS language prints values without a leading quote (`()` rather than `'()`), and older Racket versions word the arity error differently; each SOLUTION says so. Someone with DrRacket should confirm the exact text once.
- **Exact arithmetic.** M0 relies on Racket's R5RS mode returning exact `17/2` for `(/ 17 2)`, exact `100` for `(* 180 (/ 5 9))`, and exact `5` for `(sqrt 25)`. All three are standard Racket behavior but were not executed.
- **`(display …)` of dotted pairs.** The command-line alternative in the READMEs uses `display`; `(display '(((() . c) . b) . a))` prints the same dotted form as the Interactions window, so the desk-checked outputs hold for both routes.
- One README correction was made during review: M3 debug03's Think About said "two" sample calls error; three do (`record-score`, `total-score`, `top-score`).
- Error-type mix for this tree by primary type: runtime 5 (56 %), logic 4 (44 %), no compile-time errors (R5RS has none to speak of in DrRacket beyond unbound identifiers). Every activity also carries a paradigm-specific label.
