## Notes and items the lead may want to double-check

- **Determinism (`.` vs `;`).** Whether SWI prints `true.` or `true ;` for fully-bound queries such as `parent(tom, bob)` depends on JIT clause indexing. SWI 8/9 indexes on the most selective argument and answers the classic family queries deterministically, which is what the READMEs show; an older or differently configured system may offer one extra `;` that then yields `false.`. The READMEs already tell students that a trailing `false.` is harmless.
- **`bagof` with `_` (M3 debug03).** The activity depends on `bagof(N, sold(_, N), Ns)` treating the anonymous variable as a free variable and producing one bag per item (apples first, then pears, in standard order of the witness). This is standard ISO/SWI behavior but was not executed.
- **`retract/1` under the logical update view (M3 debug03).** The trace assumes SWI's logical update view (a running `retract` sees the clause list as of call time) and that `assert/1` appends at the end (`assertz` semantics). Both are SWI defaults.
- **Stack-overflow text (M2 debug03).** The exact "Stack limit (1.0Gb) exceeded" wording and depth numbers vary by SWI version and machine; SOLUTION.md says so.
- Error-type mix for this tree: 8 of 9 are logic/paradigm-specific and 1 is runtime (non-termination); no compile-time bugs. Prolog files either load or do not, and B4 asks for the M0 lesson to be the singleton warning rather than a syntax error, so this skew is expected.
